Who We Are

Reliable evidence.
Practical impact.

Aragti Consulting is a Somalia-focused consultancy providing independent third-party monitoring, research and data analytics, IT and digital solutions, and capacity building and institutional development.

We support humanitarian and development organizations, government institutions, businesses, and research organizations in understanding complex challenges, improving performance, and making informed decisions.

Our approach combines attention to local context with sound methodologies, objective analysis, and practical recommendations. We believe evidence creates the greatest value when it is clear, credible, and useful to the people making decisions.
