Our Story

Research grounded in context and purpose.

Aragti Consulting is a newly established research and data-driven consultancy based in Taleeh, Mogadishu, Somalia.

We aim to combine rigorous research standards with a practical understanding of Somalia's diverse social, economic, and institutional contexts. Our purpose is to help organizations navigate complex questions through reliable evidence, independent analysis, and clear recommendations.

We believe strong research begins with listening, proceeds through thoughtful methodology, and succeeds when findings contribute to better decisions.
