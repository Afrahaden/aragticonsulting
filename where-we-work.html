Our Geographic Focus

Research informed by
place and people.

Aragti Consulting focuses on supporting organizations in Banadir, South-west, Hirshabelle, Jubaland and Galmudug states of Somalia.

Each location presents distinct social, economic, institutional, and operational conditions. We recognize the importance of adapting research, monitoring, digital solutions, and capacity development activities to these different contexts.

Our approach emphasizes local understanding, appropriate methodologies, stakeholder engagement, and clear communication to help organizations generate reliable evidence and practical results.
